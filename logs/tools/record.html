🎙 Letter-sound recorder

Records your own voice for every sound the app uses and writes <sound>.wav straight into the folder you pick (choose the repo's audio\letters folder). Hold the record button, say the sound (not the letter name — "sss", not "ess"), release. Review with ▶, re-record as often as you like.

When you're done: run convert_letter_sounds.ps1 -NoCaps, delete the wavs, bump AUDIO_VERSION in app.js, commit. (Requires Chrome/Edge for folder access; without it, takes download to your Downloads folder instead.)
